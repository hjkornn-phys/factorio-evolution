factorio-evolution

Self-copying machines built only from base-game Factorio 2.1 parts, run headless and measured. In a colony of them, a cheaper lineage outgrows a costlier one by natural selection (light's share 0.587 against 0.380 in a cost-free control, 10 runs each, Factorio 2.1.20). In a later machine, one assembler makes all 334 parts of its own factory, drills, furnaces, oil refinery and solar field included, from ore, crude oil, water and sunlight; its copy is laid when the kit is complete, built by the machine's own two robots, and runs alone on its own solar grid (Factorio 2.1.24). The only scripted act is placing a machine's blueprint, which no vanilla mechanism can do; the colony is supplied plates and power, and the land is prepared.

The repository: films, blueprints, data, reproduction kits and an FAQ. Below: the two machines' films, then every graded colony of the selection experiment.
